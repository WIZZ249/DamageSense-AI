Make every frame count

A little preparation helps the model focus on the structure, not the conditions around it.

  • Use even daylight and avoid harsh glare or deep shadows.
  • Keep the suspected damage centered and fill most of the frame.
  • Hold the camera steady and capture the surrounding context.
  • Use a second angle when the surface or defect is hard to read.
