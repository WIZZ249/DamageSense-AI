Questions, answered

Built to support human decisions.

What does DamageSense AI assess?

It provides a first-pass structural damage classification from an uploaded or captured building image, with confidence, severity, urgency, and recommendation signals.

Is the result a replacement for an engineer?

No. The output is decision support for triage and prioritization. Human review and qualified inspection remain essential for safety-critical decisions.

Who is the platform for?

DamageSense AI is designed for humanitarian teams, field offices, emergency response coordinators, and organizations that need a faster way to organize image-based damage assessments.
